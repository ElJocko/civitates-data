## Persia

This folder holds the files for the Persia region.

For organizing cities in Civitates, the Persia region corresponds roughly to the Iranian Plateau,
bounded by the Zagros Mountains in the west,
the Persian Gulf and the Gulf of Oman in the south,
the Caucasus and the Caspian Sea in the north,
and the Amu Darya (Oxus) in the east.
